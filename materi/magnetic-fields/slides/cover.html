Magnetic Fields and Electromagnetism

Fields of force from moving charge, the force on a current, and the forces between currents.

TOPIC

20

SEQUENCE

3 meetings

LEVEL

Advanced

Open by asking: a compass needle is a tiny magnet, so what is it actually responding to? Over three meetings we build the answer: magnetic fields come from moving charge, they push on moving charge, and two currents push on each other.
