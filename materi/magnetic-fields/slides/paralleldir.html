§ 24.4 · Explaining the origin of the force

Finding the direction, step by step

Fig. 4 Top view, both currents out of the page. Teal: B₁ at wire 2. Red: force on wire 2.

01

Field of wire 1

Current out of the page: by the right-hand grip rule B₁ circles anticlockwise.

02

B₁ at the position of wire 2

Wire 2 is to the right of wire 1, where B₁ points up the page.

03

Force on wire 2, by the left-hand rule

Current out of the page in an upward field gives a force to the left: towards wire 1.

04

Wire 1, and the reversed case

Wire 1 is pushed right: they attract. Reverse one current and they repel.

This is the explanation examiners want for "explain the origin of the forces": one wire produces a field at the other; the other carries a current across that field, so it experiences a force F = BIL; the forces form a Newton’s third law pair. Students must name the rule used at each step. A neat check for step 3: field up, current out of the page, so the force is current × field = out × up, which points left.
