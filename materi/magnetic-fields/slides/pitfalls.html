Six common mistakes, corrected

No.Common mistakeCorrection
1“The force acts along the field.”The force is at right angles to both B and I.
2Measuring θ from the normal.θ is between the wire and the field; parallel gives F = 0.
3Using the whole length of wire.Only the length inside the field counts.
4Right hand for the force.Left hand for force; right-hand grip for the field of a current.
5Electron flow as the current.The rules use conventional current, opposite to electron motion.
6Field lines as real paths.They are a model; their direction and spacing describe B.
