Question 4: a current balance

A U-shaped magnet stands on a top-pan balance. A horizontal wire is clamped between its poles, with 6.0 cm of wire in the field and at right angles to it. With no current the reading is 152.40 g; with a current of 2.5 A it is 151.92 g.

PartTaskMarks
(a)Explain why the reading changes.2
(b)Calculate the magnetic flux density.3
(c)State the direction of the force on the wire.1
(d)Predict the reading if I is doubled and reversed.1

(a) The field exerts a force on the current in the wire. By Newton’s third law the wire exerts an equal and opposite force on the magnet, which changes the reading.

(b) Δm = 0.48 g = 4.8 × 10⁻⁴ kg; F = 4.8 × 10⁻⁴ × 9.81 = 4.71 × 10⁻³ N
B = F ÷ IL = 4.71 × 10⁻³ ÷ (2.5 × 0.060) = 0.031 T

(c) The reading falls, so the force on the magnet is upwards; the force on the wire is therefore downwards.

(d) The force doubles and reverses, pushing the magnet down by the weight of 0.96 g: 152.40 + 0.96 = 153.36 g.
