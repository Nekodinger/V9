A solenoid 25 cm long has 500 turns and carries 1.2 A. (a) Calculate B inside it, away from the ends. [2] (b) An iron rod is pushed inside. State and explain the effect on B. [2]

A straight horizontal wire with mass per unit length 0.020 kg m⁻¹ lies at right angles to a horizontal field of 0.080 T. Find the current that lets it float, the magnetic force balancing its weight. [3]

(a) n = 500 ÷ 0.25 = 2000 m⁻¹
B = μ₀nI = 4π × 10⁻⁷ × 2000 × 1.2 = 3.0 × 10⁻³ T

(b) B increases greatly: the iron is magnetised by the solenoid field, its domains line up and add their own field.

BIL = mg ⟹ I = (m/L)g ÷ B
I = 0.020 × 9.81 ÷ 0.080 = 2.5 A

The current must flow in the direction that gives an upward force by Fleming’s left-hand rule. The length L cancels.
