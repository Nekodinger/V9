02

Force on a current-carrying conductor

Why does a wire in a magnetic field move? How big is the force, which way does it act, and how do we use it to define and measure B?

Starter demo if available: a loose length of wire (or aluminium foil strip) between the poles of a strong U-shaped magnet jumps when a current from a low-voltage supply is switched on. Reverse the current or the magnet and it jumps the other way.
