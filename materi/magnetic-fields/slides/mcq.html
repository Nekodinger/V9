A wire 0.20 m long carries 4.0 A at 60° to a field of 0.050 T. What is the force on it?

Which is the tesla expressed in SI base units?

Parallel wires X and Y carry I and 3I in the same direction. The force on X is F. What is the force on Y?

A 2.0 × 10⁻² N
B 3.5 × 10⁻² N
C 4.0 × 10⁻² N
D 4.6 × 10⁻² N

A kg s⁻² A⁻¹
B kg m s⁻² A⁻¹
C kg A s⁻²
D kg m² s⁻² A⁻¹

A F/3, attractive
B F, attractive
C 3F, attractive
D F, repulsive

B. F = 0.050 × 4.0 × 0.20 × sin 60° = 0.0346 N. A uses cos 60°; C ignores the angle.

A. T = N A⁻¹ m⁻¹ = (kg m s⁻²) ÷ (A m) = kg s⁻² A⁻¹.

B. The forces are a Newton’s third law pair, so they are equal. Like currents attract.
