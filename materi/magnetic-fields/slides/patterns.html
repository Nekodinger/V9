These patterns were computed, not drawn by hand, so the shapes are realistic. Exam sketches need: arrows on every line, lines leaving N and entering S, closer lines near the poles, and no crossing lines. For attraction, lines run straight across the gap. For repulsion, lines are pushed sideways and there is a neutral point X midway. Ask: why is X exactly midway? Because the magnets are identical.
