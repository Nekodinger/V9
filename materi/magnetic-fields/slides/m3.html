Magnetic fields due to currents

How strong is the field near a wire or inside a solenoid, and why do two currents push or pull on each other?

Recap question to open: a wire carries a current in a magnetic field. What two things set the direction of the force? Then bridge: a current makes a field, and a current in a field feels a force, so two currents must exert forces on each other.
