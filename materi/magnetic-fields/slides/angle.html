§ 20.2 · When the wire is not perpendicular

F = BIL sin θ

θ is the angle between the wire (the current) and the field.

01

along the wire: B cos θ, no force
across the wire: B sin θ, force

02

F = (B sin θ) IL = BIL sin θ

03

θ = 90°: F = BIL, the maximum.
θ = 0° or 180°: F = 0, wire along the field.

Equivalent view, shown in the animation: keep B whole and use only the part of the wire perpendicular to B, which has length L sin θ. Either way F = BIL sin θ. Watch the sine graph: the force is largest at 90° and falls to zero when the wire lies along the field. Common error: measuring θ from the normal to the field, which turns sin into cos.
