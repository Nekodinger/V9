Learning outcomes

Three meetings, one idea: moving charge

01

Concept of a magnetic field

Describe a magnetic field as a field of force from moving charges or permanent magnets. Use field lines to represent a field and sketch patterns.

02

Force on a current-carrying conductor

Determine the size and direction of the force. Define magnetic flux density and know how it can be measured.

03

Magnetic fields due to currents

Explain the origin of the forces between current-carrying conductors, find their direction and calculate their size.
