The animation switches the current direction every six seconds. Ask students to predict the circulation before it flips, using the grip rule with their own right hand. The rings are spaced so that their density falls as 1/r; Meeting 3 turns this into B = μ0 I / (2πr). Note the arrows also move more slowly further out, another cue that B is weaker there.
