§ 24.1 · Drawing in three dimensions

Dots, crosses and uniform fields

DOT

Out of the page: the tip of the arrow coming towards you.

CROSS

Into the page: the tail feathers moving away.

The same symbols are used for currents and for fields.

UNIFORM FIELD

N pole (left) · S pole (right)

Parallel, equally spaced, straight lines: the same B everywhere. Found between flat pole pieces and inside a long solenoid.
