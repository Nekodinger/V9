§ 24.1 · Origin of magnetic fields

Every magnetic field comes from moving charge

Fig. 1 Conventional current and the loops of B around it

01 · CURRENTS

Charge carriers drift along a wire. The field forms closed loops around the current.

02 · PERMANENT MAGNETS

Electron spin and orbital motion act as tiny current loops. In iron they line up in domains and their fields add.

The animation shows positive (conventional) charge carriers moving to the right and loops of B around the wire. Point out that the loops have no start or end: magnetic field lines always form closed loops, which is why there are no isolated magnetic poles. Electron spin is a quantum property; at this level it is enough to treat each electron as a tiny current loop.
