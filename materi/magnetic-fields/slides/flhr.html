The diagram is a perspective view: the current arrow points towards you, the field points right and the force points up. Check it against the catapult slide: field right, current into the page gave a downward force, so current out of the page gives an upward force. The rule uses conventional current; for a beam of electrons the current points opposite to their motion. The vector form is beyond the syllabus but useful for students who also take Further Mathematics.
