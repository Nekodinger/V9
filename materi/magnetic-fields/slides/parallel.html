§ 20.4 · Forces between currents

Two parallel wires

Each wire sits in the magnetic field of the other, so each feels a force F = BIL.

LIKE CURRENTS

attract

UNLIKE CURRENTS

repel

For like currents the field lines wrap round both wires and draw them together; for unlike currents they crowd between the wires and push them apart. This is the reverse of charges and poles.
