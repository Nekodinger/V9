§ 20.1 · Concept of a magnetic field

A third field of force

A magnetic field is a region in which a moving charge, a current-carrying conductor or a magnetic material experiences a force.

FieldProduced byActs onStrengthUnit
Gravitationalmassmassg = F / mN kg⁻¹
ElectricchargechargeE = F / QN C⁻¹
Magneticmoving charge: currents, magnetsmoving charge: currents, magnetic materialsB = F / (IL)T

The essential difference: a charge at rest feels no magnetic force, however strong the field.
