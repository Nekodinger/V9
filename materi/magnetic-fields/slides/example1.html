Worked example 1

A wire at an angle to the field

PROBLEM

A straight wire of length 40 cm carries a current of 3.0 A. It lies at 30° to a uniform magnetic field of flux density 0.25 T.

(a) Calculate the force on the wire.

(b) Find the largest possible force for this wire and current, and the angle at which it acts.

(c) State the direction of the force.

SOLUTION

(a) Convert first: L = 0.40 m

F = BIL sin θ = 0.25 × 3.0 × 0.40 × sin 30°
F = 0.15 N

(b) Largest when sin θ = 1, so θ = 90°

Fmax = BIL = 0.25 × 3.0 × 0.40
Fmax = 0.30 N at θ = 90°

(c) Direction

Perpendicular to the plane containing the wire and the field; Fleming’s left-hand rule gives which side.
